Project Team

A collaboration in physics education, engineering education, augmented reality development, and research on student learning.

MM

Michele W. McColgan

Siena University · Physics & Astronomy

Professor of Physics & Astronomy and lead developer of MARVLS. Her research examines how augmented-reality visualizations can support conceptual understanding, spatial visualization, representational fluency, and problem solving in physics and engineering education.

GH

George E. Hassel

Siena University · Physics & Astronomy

Teaching Professor of Physics & Astronomy whose work includes active and inquiry-based approaches to undergraduate physics instruction and the classroom implementation and study of MARVLS learning activities.

KP

Kamyar Pashayi

Siena University · Applied Physics

Associate Professor with engineering and applied-physics expertise. His research interests include the application of augmented reality in engineering and physics education and the development of interactive learning experiences.

JM

Jason W. Morphew

Purdue University · Engineering Education

Assistant Professor in Engineering Education whose research applies cognitive science and learning-science principles to the design and evaluation of learning environments and technologies that support STEM learning and engagement.

Collaboration

Connecting educational technology and learning research

The project brings together AR design and development, undergraduate physics instruction, engineering education, and research frameworks for examining how students coordinate mathematical, physical, symbolic, spatial, and embodied representations.
